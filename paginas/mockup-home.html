Why 316L, and not "stainless steel" alone

Most jewelry marketed as "stainless steel" is actually plated base metal — it looks right in the photo and fades in six weeks. That's the complaint behind almost every 1-star review in this category.

  • ✓ 316L releases far less nickel than 304-grade — safer for sensitive skin
  • ✓ Solid steel through and through, not a coating over something cheaper
  • ✓ Hypoallergenic, tarnish-resistant, built to outlast the trend
